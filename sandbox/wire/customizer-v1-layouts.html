Wireframe. Three layouts of the live customizer: desktop 1440 x 900 and phone 390 x 844 on the Your Design item, plus the phone on the last item (Card). Grey = block, dashed = door, dark = the one primary.

My pick: A. My Designs and More gifts change the gift itself, so they sit on the gift; they stay in one place on all three items, cost no row of the ~300 px phone sheet, and the panel keeps its live order with one primary.

The same in all three: stepper labels Design · Box · Card, help as one text door under the primary, and on Card a summary line above Add to cart.
