Buttons

One class, three types, three colours, three size ladders and five states. Every button on this page is live: it runs on the same stylesheet the product will, and the numbers in the anatomy table are read back out of the rendered page at your current window width.

Nine cells, three sizes each, and one stylesheet behind all twenty seven. The markup of a cell is the base class plus three words: gb-btn gb-btn--l gb-btn--outline gb-btn--secondary. Filled, primary and L are the defaults, so a bare gb-btn is the top left cell of the L column.

02 · The icon

On either side, or on both, or on neither

An icon is a span with one glyph in it, and which side it stands on is DOM order and nothing else. There is no left modifier and no right modifier, because the row already answers that question and a second way to say it would be a second thing to keep in step. The glyph takes the current ink, so it changes colour with every state for free.

03 · States

Rest, hover, focus, active and disabled

Hover always darkens. That is a rule and not a preference: a button that lightens under the pointer reads as switched off, and it is the one thing about our buttons the live product has already corrected us on. Focus is a ring rather than a colour change, so it can sit on top of any of the five other states without arguing with them, and it appears for the keyboard only.

04 · Anatomy

What each ladder measures right now

Three ladders, and each one moves with the width of the screen as well as the size of the button. The table is measured, not typed: three hidden buttons are read with getComputedStyle every time the window changes size, so what you see below is what this browser is rendering at this moment.

The full ladders, with their thresholds: S is 36, 42 from 1280 and 48 from 2000, off the public header. L is 48, 56 and 64 on the same thresholds, off the guest sign in submit, with a label that also steps at 640. XL is 56, 64 and 72, off the product page Continue, with a label that steps at 768. Vertical padding is a flat 8 on all three, which is what live measures at every width and every size.

A note on the proportion formula of 17.08, pad-y = 0.24H and pad-x = 0.5H with the gap equal to pad-y. It still describes the small end well: 36 gives 8.6, which rounds to the live 8. Above that, live and the formula part company. The live form submit and the live product CTA both hold pad-y at 8 and run their gaps two or three times wider than that. Where live has measured a number, live wins and the formula is the tie breaker for the sizes live has never shown. Both readings are kept in tokens.css so the decision stays visible.

05 · Where it lives

The classes this organism replaces

Four button classes are in use across the prototypes today, each written for the screen it was needed on. The organism was calibrated against all four by instrument at 1280, 1920 and 2258. Nothing has been migrated yet: this is the map for phase two, once the organism has been approved.

Class todayWhere it livesBecomesWhat changes for it
.gbh-cta Header, Book a meeting --s --filled --primary Nothing. The two match to the pixel at all three widths, label included.
.gbh-cta--outline Header, My portal --s --outline --secondary Nothing. Geometry, ink, hairline and hover wash all match.
.gbh-cta--inverted The dark mobile panel and the transparent header --s --outline --inverse Nothing. The inverse colour was built out of these live values.
.gba-btn The guest sign in form and the hub shelf --l --filled --primary It loses the browser default border it never asked for. Full width and the top margin are page layout and stay with the page.
.btn-xl Checkout, Place order --xl --filled --primary Its label grows from 11 to the live 14 to 16. Today it wears the small header label on the largest button in the system, which is the mismatch we were already caught on once.
.gsp-btn The portal side panel --l --filled --primary It gains the label span it has never had, and its grey disabled step gives way to the system fade.

Phase two is a separate pass and needs its own approval: every consumer above is a page people are looking at right now, and two of them are prototypes in front of the client. The organism does not touch them until then.
