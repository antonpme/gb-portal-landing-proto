Field Stable

One control in five looks: the underlined input of the live sign in, the floating label of the live lead form, the phone row, the six cell code and the password with an eye.

Window width right now: measuring. The field has two height ladders and they do not step together.

Overview

One field, five looks, two owners

A field is an underline, not a box: no frame, no ground and no radius anywhere in the family. Everything below is that one control seen from a different side. Four of the five come from auth.css, which owns the field; the phone row comes from booking.css, which owns the only place two controls share one underline. Nothing here is new: the looks were built between 18 and 28 August, and this wave stands them up and measures them.

States

What changes, and to what

The table follows the workbench: it is filled by measuring the specimens standing above it, so it always describes the look on the stage and never a general case. The field changes three things and the line is the loudest of them: it goes blue under the pointer, blue under the caret and red when the answer is wrong. The label and the ink stay where they are, and there is no ring, no ground and no shadow anywhere in the family.

StateLineInkLabelToken

Focus is DECLARED, and it is the one value on this page live did not give us. Until 28 August the field answered the pointer and ignored the caret: hover took the line to Blue 600, focus only faded the placeholder. Ton, the same day: «при постановке курсора полоса не становится активной; должно быть как у обычного TextInput». The live lead form did not render in any frame that day, so there was nothing to measure, and by Ton-17 an obvious gap in live is closed here and live catches up later. Focus took the hover colour, because blue is state (Ton-5) and a focused field is the most stated a field gets. One rule, both controls. At the next harvest of the live form, read the focused border-bottom-color off an input and a textarea and bring it back here.

Disabled is the state the system has still not decided. The disabled specimen is a plain disabled control wearing whatever the browser gives it, because no live screen has ever shown us a disabled field, and inventing one quietly is the thing this page exists to prevent. On the list for Ton.

Sizes

Two ladders, and they do not climb together

The field has two height ladders, not one, and that is not drift: they were measured on two different pages of the same client. The guest sign in walks the control family 48, 56, 64, and its type grows 14, 16, 18. The lead form of the live contact page stays at 48 through 1280 and only reaches 56 at 2000, with type at 13, 14, 16. A Live page wears the ladder of the live page it imitates, so both are right and neither may be tidied into the other.

The textarea has a floor rather than a height, because the number of lines decides the rest.

With label

Three caps labels, and one open question

Every look but the floating one puts a small caps label over the line, and the system has one caps voice for that: the Eyebrow role, 12 at 600 with 0.12em of tracking in Zinc 500. On 27 August the field label moved onto it, a documented departure from the live sign in, which sets its own 11 at 0.5px. Measured today the field label and the Eyebrow are the same voice to the hundredth. The label of the code step is the one that did not come along.

The question for Ton. Two of the three agree and the third does not. .gba-vlabel, the label over the six code cells, is the same size, the same weight and the same ink as the Eyebrow, and carries half its tracking. It also prints the gold asterisk unconditionally, with no way for a consumer to say the field is optional. The copy rule of the system says caps labels are the Eyebrow role and that there is one caps voice; either the code label joins it, which is two lines at its owner and a visible change to the sign in flow, or the exception is declared and written down. This wave measured it and did not touch it.

The floating look has no caps label at all. At rest its label is the hint, in Inter 300 at the size of the input; when the field is used it shrinks to 11.2px and rises four pixels above the line. 11.2 is not invented: the live rule is 70 per cent of the parent, the parent is the 16px body text of that page, and the result is the same 11.2 at every width. One field of the live lead form keeps a static label over the line anyway, the comment, and it is the one place in the house where a label over a field is not in caps: the live computed style says so on all four widths, whatever the class in their markup claims.

Properties

The attributes, and the drawer that reads a specimen

Two layers, the way the mature systems keep them. The table is the whole surface of the component, readable and linkable in one piece. The drawer answers a different question, about one specimen: the Properties button in the corner of any specimen on this page opens it with the geometry, the type, the colours and the label measured off that element, with the token that produced each value beside it.

AttributeTypeDefaultWhat it does
input-idstringrequiredTies the label to the input. Every field needs one, and it has to be unique on the page
namestringrequiredThe name the form submits under
typestringtextAny input type, plus textarea for the multi line version, and password, which is the one that takes an eye
labelstringrequiredThe label. Sentence case in the markup: the caps belong to the role, not to the copy
placeholderstringemptyThe hint inside the line. It fades out on focus, which is what live does. A floating single line field ignores it and takes one space of its own instead, because :placeholder-shown does not fire on an empty string and the label would never rise
optionalbooleanoffMarks the field as not required. Required is the default, which is why the modifier names the exception, and it is the switch the gold asterisk reads
label-stylestringemptyfloating puts the label inside the control and lets it rise. It changes one thing in the markup, the position of the label after the control, because the rise is CSS on a sibling and not a line of script
eyebooleanoffAdds the reveal button in the corner of the control. It flips type between password and text and does nothing else
valuestringemptyThe value the field opens with
autocompletestringthe nameHanded straight to the control, so the browser can fill it. A floating label rises for a browser fill too, since Chrome clears :placeholder-shown
wrap-modstringemptyAn extra class on the wrapper, for the spacing a form needs: .gba-field--flow is the 24 / 32 / 40 rhythm of the live flow, .gba-field--last is the last field of a group
el.setError(text)methodnonePuts the control in error and prints the sentence under it; clearError() takes both away. This is how the specimens above go red: the page does not write the error markup itself
.is-hover .is-focusclassoffDemo only, the showcase. Hold a state still so a row of them can stand side by side

The phone row and the code have no element of their own. The phone is markup inside the booking flow, because two controls share one underline and one row; the code is six inputs the flow drives as a single control, with paste and auto advance. Both are shown here as their owner writes them, character for character. If either ever gets a second consumer, that is the day it becomes an element.

Usage

Which look, and when

DoDo not
Reach for the underline with a caps label when the form owns the page and has room above every lineMix the two looks in one form. One form, one look: two labels behaving differently in one column reads as a bug
Reach for the floating label in a drawer, a panel or a dense column, where a row of labels would cost more height than the fieldsUse the floating label for a field a person has to re read while it is full. Once it is filled the label is 11.2px above the line, a reminder rather than a heading
Label every field, in sentence case, and let the role put the caps onUse the placeholder as the label. It disappears the moment someone types, in both looks
Say what is wrong under the field, in one sentence, through setError()Colour the line red and leave the person to guess what it means
Mark the optional ones, since required is the defaultMark the required ones with an asterisk. There would be one on almost every field
Let a textarea grow with its content, and keep its resize handle verticalGive a textarea a horizontal handle. It breaks the column of the form, which is why the organism allows one axis
Put the error under the field it belongs toMove the error into a tooltip or a toast, or collect all of them at the top of the form. It has to stand where the answer is being typed

Ton, 27 August, on the form we imitate: «Раз мы имитируем live, должно быть как там». That is why the floating ladder is not the guest ladder, why the phone line is a step darker than the four fields beside it, and why the error sentences of the booking read in the live wording. Copy quoted from a live page is quoted whole; the rules of our own copy apply to the words we write.

Accessibility

Label, error, focus and target size

LabelEvery field carries a real label with for pointing at the control's id, in both looks. A floating label is still a label: it moves, it does not stop existing, and it is never replaced by a placeholder
ErrorsetError() sets aria-invalid="true" on the control and prints the sentence in a role="alert", so it is announced when it appears. Tying the two together with aria-describedby is the gap here: the sentence is announced but it is not yet named as the description of the field, and closing it is a change at the owner
FocusVisible on every look since 28 August: the line goes Blue 600 when the caret arrives. Declared, not measured, and the one behaviour on this page live does not have yet
DisabledThe native attribute, on the control. What it should look like is undecided, so what a person sees today is the browser's own grey
AutofillEvery field takes autocomplete and defaults it to the field name. The floating label rises for a browser fill as well as for a keystroke, because the rise reads :placeholder-shown and not an input event
The codeSix cells, each with a name of its own, Digit 1 to Digit 6, and inputmode="numeric" so a phone opens the number pad. A paste into any cell fills all six
The eyeA real button with an aria-label, not a glyph on a div, and it sits inside the field's own box, so a tab reaches it right after the control
Target sizeThe shortest control in the family is 48 tall at every width, which clears the 44 a touch target needs. The eye is an 18px glyph in the corner and is the one thing under it
Code

One element, and the attributes on it

Four of the five looks are one element and its attributes. The phone is the one that is markup, and it is markup because two controls share a row.

<!-- the default: underline, caps label over the line -->
<gb-field input-id="email" name="email" type="email"
          label="Work email" placeholder="you@company.com"></gb-field>

<!-- multi line, and not required -->
<gb-field input-id="brief" name="brief" type="textarea"
          label="What are you looking for?" optional></gb-field>

<!-- the floating label, with the rhythm of the live flow -->
<gb-field input-id="company" name="company" type="text"
          label="Company Name" label-style="floating"
          wrap-mod="gba-field--flow" optional></gb-field>

<!-- the password, with the eye -->
<gb-field input-id="pass" name="password" type="password"
          label="Password" placeholder="Set your password"
          autocomplete="new-password" eye></gb-field>
