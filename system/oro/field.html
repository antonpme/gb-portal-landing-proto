States

Six states, and one the system has not decided

The live field has no focus ring. Focus does exactly one thing on the product: it fades the placeholder out and leaves the underline where it was. Hover is the only step that moves the line, and it moves it to blue. That is not an omission on our side, it is what the instrument read, and the showcase shows it rather than improving on it quietly. Click any field to read its properties.

Properties
AttributeTypeDefaultWhat it does
input-idstringrequiredTies the label to the input. Every field needs one, and it has to be unique on the page
namestringrequiredThe name the form submits under
typestringtextAny input type, plus textarea for the multi line version
labelstringrequiredThe caps label over the line. Sentence case in the markup
placeholderstringemptyThe hint inside the line. It fades out on focus, which is what live does
optionalbooleanoffMarks the field as not required. Required is the default, which is why the modifier names the exception
.is-hover .is-focusclassoffDemo only, this page. Hold a state still for a screenshot
Usage
DoDo not
