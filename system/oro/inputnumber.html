Variants

Two sizes, two wells, and who wears which

The frame does not have a ladder of its own: it is exactly as tall as the two buttons inside it, which is the only way the two can never disagree. What varies is which rung of the icon button it is built from, and how wide the number's well is.

States

Four states, and the two that belong to the range

Rest, hover and focus belong to the frame; at the floor and at the ceiling the state belongs to the number, and it is the button that shows it. A stepper that hides the minus at its floor moves the plus under the pointer, and the next click adds a gift nobody asked for; so the minus stays where it is and goes out.

Sizes

The frame is as tall as its buttons

A circle carries no label, so the icon button does not ride the label ladder: its rungs are 40, 48 and 56 at every width. The stepper inherits exactly that, and the only thing on it that steps with the window is the number, which rides the field ladder because it is read and typed like a field value.

Anatomy

Five parts, three of them borrowed

PartWhat it isMeasured
Frame.gb-stepper: a hairline, a radius and a clip. It sets no height of its own
Minus and plus.gb-btn--icon --s --plain, the button organism. The stepper restyles neither
Glyphsminus and plus from the icon record, filled in by stepper.js when the markup leaves the box empty
Well.gb-stepper__value: a cell between two seams. An <input type="number">, or a <span> where nothing is typed
SeamsZinc 200 inside a Zinc 300 frame: the outer line is the control, the inner ones are a join within it
Properties
NameWhere it goesWhat it does
.gb-stepperthe frameThe whole control: hairline, radius, clip, hover and focus
--densethe frameNarrows the well to the button's own width, for a table row
--mthe frameThe 48 rung. The two buttons take --m with it
.is-disabled / aria-disabledthe frameThe whole control off: the line steps back and both buttons go out
data-step="-1" / "1"the two buttonsHow the component finds them, and by how much each one moves
data-gb-stepper="host"the frameThe host owns the value. The component stops writing numbers and does bounds and aria only. The checkout's four wear this
min / maxthe numberThe floor and the ceiling. On a span well they are data-min and data-max on the frame
data-step-bythe frameHow much one press is worth, when it is not one
gbst:changeevent, from the frameFired after the component moves a value: { value, min, max }

The Properties button in the corner of a specimen reads it into the drawer. The table is read whole; the drawer answers about one control.

Usage
DoDo not
Use a stepper where the number is small and changed by one or two at a timeUse it where the number is typed in full. Past two digits a person types, and a field is the control for typing
Give it a floor, and let the minus go out when it is reachedHide the minus at the floor. The plus slides under the pointer and the next click counts something nobody asked for
Say what is being counted, in a label beside it or on the frameLeave a bare number in a row and trust the column heading to explain it
Leave the well typeable when a person may jump to a bigger numberMake the well read only and then set a ceiling far away, so the only route to it is thirty clicks
Let the host own the value when the number means more than itself: a pool, a total, a recountWire a host handler and leave the component driving as well. Two owners count twice
Accessibility
GroupingThe frame is role="group" with a name, so the two buttons and the number are announced as one thing
KeyboardTab reaches the minus, the number and the plus. On a number input the arrows are the browser's own and they already honour min and max; on a span well the component gives the same two keys and the whole spinbutton contract with them
The buttonsEach carries its own aria-label, because a glyph is not a name. Both glyphs are aria-hidden
BoundsReal disabled, not a grey paint: at the floor the minus is out of the tab order and out of the accessibility tree's action list
Target size40 by 40 per button at the S rung, 48 at M. Nothing in the control goes under 40
Code

The frame, two buttons and a well. Leave the glyph boxes empty and the component fills them from the icon record; write a glyph in by hand and it is left alone.

<!-- the component owns the value -->
<div class="gb-stepper" aria-label="Quantity">
  <button class="gb-btn gb-btn--icon gb-btn--s gb-btn--ghost gb-btn--secondary gb-btn--plain"
          type="button" data-step="-1" aria-label="Decrease quantity">
    <span class="gb-btn__icon"></span>
  </button>
  <input class="gb-stepper__value" type="number" min="1" value="1" aria-label="Quantity">
  <button class="gb-btn gb-btn--icon gb-btn--s gb-btn--ghost gb-btn--secondary gb-btn--plain"
          type="button" data-step="1" aria-label="Increase quantity">
    <span class="gb-btn__icon"></span>
  </button>
</div>

<!-- the host owns the value: bounds and aria only -->
<div class="gb-stepper gb-stepper--dense" data-gb-stepper="host" aria-label="Gifts">

</div>
