Design system

Typography

Two families, one root size and a ladder of roles that steps as the screen widens. Every role stands here as a live specimen at its real size, the numbers beside it are read back out of the rendered page at your current window width, and any specimen opens its properties on a click.

Window width right now: measuring, which puts the ladders on the measuring rung. The type ladders step at 640, 768, 1280 and 2000.

Foundation

Typefaces Stable

Two families and one root. Noto Serif is the title voice and nothing else; Inter carries every other word in the product.

Title family--font-serif, Noto Serif with Georgia behind it. Titles and title numerals only
Text family--font-sans, Inter with the system stack behind it. Body, leads, captions, labels, fields, tables, everything that is not a title
Root--font-size-root, 16px. The reading size of the product, and the size a paragraph returns to whenever nothing else applies
Thresholds640, 768, 1280 and 2000. The wide rung is the client's own 2xl at 2000 and not the framework's 1536; the widest live figures were read on a 2258 screen and land on that rung
OwnerThe families and the root are tokens in system/assets/tokens.css. The role level is studio/system/TYPE-SCALE.md, and only a few roles have a class yet

One rule decides every disagreement on this page: a measurement beats a drawing. Where the instrument has read a value off the running product, that value is the role, and the design file is a source of names rather than numbers. Where the product has never shown a size, the drawing is used and the row says so out loud. Nothing here is interpolated quietly, and nothing is rounded to make a ladder look tidy. The unit is the pixel, because that is the unit the system was measured in.

Roles

Scale Counting

The whole ladder, largest first, in seven groups. Each role is a specimen at the size it really is, with its class, its family, its numbers and its provenance beside it. Resize the window and every specimen and every figure moves with the ladder.

View

Two kinds of row live in this ladder and the difference matters more than any single number. A role with a class is worn by a real stylesheet: the specimen wears the class, the instrument reads what the browser drew, and if the organism ever drifts from the record the page shows it. A role without a class has no stylesheet to read, so its specimen is drawn from the recorded ladder and the instrument reads that back. Those are marked, and they are the working list of what the system still owes itself. The copy button beside a role hands over the declaration it is standing on.

Usage

Pairing rules

Which voice, at which size, in which case. Rules only: a role is chosen by what the text does on the screen, never by what some other screen happens to look like.

The rules, short

The serif is a title voice. Page titles, section titles and the large numerals that behave as titles. It does not take a paragraph, a caption, a label, a table cell or a form. One serif level per screen level; the level under it is Inter, which carries everything else.

Nothing in content goes under 14. A size at the floor keeps a reading ink and never drops to a pale grey to soften it. Two marks live below the floor and are not a licence for a third: the badge numeral, and the small button label.

Caps belongs to two roles, the eyebrow and the button label. Everywhere else the text is sentence case, headings included, and two cases inside one screen are a defect rather than a style.

The step between two roles has to be visible without measuring it. Weight is a second axis and not a substitute for size: a bolder line at the same size reads as emphasis inside a level, not as a level above it. Line length stays near the reading measure, around 80 characters, and the tracking of a role is never adjusted by hand at the place it is used.

The same four rules, set

Each pair below is real layout, not a picture of one, and both halves are drawn from the recorded ladder. The line under each half says why, once.

This is the reading size of the product. A paragraph at this rung runs long enough to turn several lines, which is the only way to judge whether the line height is doing its job, and it keeps a dark ink so that nothing about it says it does not matter.

A caption under the paragraph, one step down.

measuring

What the rung switch changes is honest and limited. The title, the lead, the section, the body and the caption have no class in the system, so they are drawn from the recorded ladder and they redraw at whichever rung you pick. The eyebrow and the button are real organisms whose ladders live in a stylesheet, and a stylesheet keys off the window, so those two stay at the rung this window is on. The column width does not move either: only the window can move that, and resizing the window moves everything at once.

Properties

What the drawer reports

Click any specimen on this page and the drawer opens with these seven values, measured off that element, with the token beside each one that has a token.

PropertyWhere the value comes fromToken, when there is one
FamilyThe first family the browser resolved on the element--font-serif or --font-sans
SizeComputed at the current window width, so it names the rung the window is onOnly the roles that have one: the root, the eyebrow, the button label rungs
WeightComputed off the element--eyebrow-weight and the button label weight
Line heightComputed. A role whose line height was never recorded reads as normal, which is the truth about itNone. The system has no line height tokens
TrackingComputed, in pixels, whatever unit the role was recorded in--eyebrow-tracking and the button label tracking
CaseComputed. Only the eyebrow and the button label return uppercaseNone. Case is a property of the role
InkComputed. Most roles record type only and inherit the ink of the surface they stand onThe zinc scale, and the blue of a state

The drawer also names the role, its provenance and its class. Where a role says role without a class yet, the drawer says the same thing and shows the recorded ladder instead of markup to copy, because there is nothing yet to copy. That is the honest state of the system and the reason this page exists in this shape. The copy button on a role hands over the same declaration in CSS, with a token wherever a token holds at every width.
